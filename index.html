How it works

Discover, pair once, then send.

Set up takes under a minute. After the first pairing, your devices simply recognise each other.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Open LocoDrop on both devices. Nearby devices on the same network appear in seconds.

  2. 2

    Pair

    Scan a QR code once to establish trust. Keys are exchanged directly between the two devices.

  3. 3

    Send

    Pick files, folders, or a whole batch. They travel straight across your local network, encrypted.

Why LocoDrop

Built to stay out of the cloud.

Private by design

Transfers are encrypted end to end and run directly between your devices. File contents are never uploaded, mined, or logged.

No cloud

Files move over your local network, not through anyone's servers. Fast on Wi-Fi, and it works with the internet down.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no password. Pair your devices once and start sending. Core features are free, forever.

Every platform, first class

The painful pairs, iPhone to Windows, Android to Mac, are the ones LocoDrop is built for. No platform is a second-class citizen.
