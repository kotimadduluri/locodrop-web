How it works

Discover, pair once, then send.

Set up takes under a minute. After the first pairing, your devices simply recognise each other.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Open LocoDrop on both devices. Nearby devices on the same network appear in seconds, no setup wizard, no typing IP addresses.

  2. 2

    Pair

    Scan a QR code once to establish trust. Keys are exchanged directly between the two devices and stay on them.

  3. 3

    Send

    Pick files, a folder, or a whole batch. They travel straight across your local network, encrypted the entire way.

Questions

The short answers.

Everything you'd want to know before your first send.

Is LocoDrop really free?

Yes. Sending files between your own devices is free, forever, no account and no subscription for the essentials. If we ever add pro extras, the core transfer stays free.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Files move directly across your local network, so they keep working even when the internet is down. If both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, that's all you need. And if there's no Wi-Fi at all, make one phone a hotspot, connect the other to it, and you can still send files.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up, no email, no password. You pair two devices once by scanning a QR code, and from then on they recognise each other automatically.

How is it different from AirDrop or a cloud drive?

AirDrop stops at the edge of Apple's ecosystem, and cloud drives upload your files to someone else's servers first. LocoDrop works across iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Linux, and your files never leave your own network.

Is it actually secure?

Every transfer is encrypted end to end and runs straight between your paired devices. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or logged along the way, because there's no server in the path to store it.

Which platforms are supported?

iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Linux, with the same one-tap send on each. Early access is rolling out platform by platform, join the list and we'll tell you when yours is ready.
